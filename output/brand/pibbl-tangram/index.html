Use it consistently.

Preserve proportions and color order. Leave clear space equal to at least one eighth of the mark's painted height. White and color-on-dark masters are transparent and need a dark backdrop.

This is a clean vector reconstruction of the selected raster concept. The lettering is custom path artwork; no font installation is needed. Colors are sampled flat values, with the source image's shading removed. In version 1.1, the blue bowl's bottom aligns exactly with the coral rectangle's top.
