Textures in motion

Animation changes the marble, paper, and cellular textures above. Below, the moving stirrer drags the existing ink into a wake. The reaction keeps branching and reshaping it. Pause the reaction to isolate the stirrer’s displacement.

Uses CRC’s public texture, animation, and physics APIs. Texture computation and Rapier physics run in separate WASM memories; small movement messages connect them. Animation remains on CRC’s existing scheduler.
